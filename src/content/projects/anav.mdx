<CaseSection title="Objective">
  An autonomous UAV for a Martian-surface landing scenario, built for an ISRO competition. It takes
  off on its own, scans the terrain, and searches for a safe landing zone, then approaches and
  touches down with no human input.
</CaseSection>

  <p>
    The vehicle builds a hazard map from the camera and IMU, scores candidate sites for flatness,
    clearance, and approach margin, and selects the safest. It records telemetry and its decisions
    throughout the mission. The public repository is the browser ground dashboard: live telemetry
    over WebSocket beside the camera stream.
  </p>
</CaseSection>
