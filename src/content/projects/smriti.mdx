<CaseSection title="Objective">
  Continuity at home for an older adult, without handing their life to a vendor cloud. SMRITI is not
  a medical device: it does not diagnose anything, medicine taps are self-reported only, and the
  older adult never sees a score.
</CaseSection>

  <p>
    Both phones talk to the laptop over the home Wi‑Fi. Pairing uses a short code or a QR code, so
    the older adult never needs an email address.
  </p>
</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="Constraints">
  Every record says who reported it and when. When two reports disagree (for example, a relative saw
  the tablets still in the box but the companion phone logged them as taken), the app flags it for
  review instead of choosing one.
</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="Measurements">
  <p>
    After a play session, an optional local model suggests a gentler or fuller game pace. It came
    out of a Smart India Hackathon 2026 problem on cognitive gaming and memory support for older
    adults with dementia in India's North Eastern Region. It was trained and tested on synthetic
    play events only: a research prototype, not a diagnosis.
  </p>
