<CaseSection title="Objective">
  Bharatiya Antariksh Hackathon 2026, Problem Statement 10: colourise and enhance infrared satellite
  imagery so objects are easier to interpret. A single-band thermal tile goes in; a sharpened
  infrared image, a colour interpretation, and a per-pixel risk map come out. The risk map marks
  where the colour is not supported by the input.
</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="Constraints">
  The challenge allows only the thermal band at inference, so anything else can guide training but
  never ship. Patches are split by scene and date, so no scene appears in both training and test.
</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="Failures and iterations">
  <p>
    The baseline, trained on pixel loss alone, painted every land cover the same brown. Temperature
    does not determine colour, so the loss rewarded the average. That failure motivated the separate
    enhancement stage, the semantic head, and the risk map.
  </p>
  <p>
    Distilling the teacher into a thermal-only student raised PSNR, but its hallucination proxy was
    0.121392 against 0.023902, so it was rejected. On the pilot split, the largest model tried
    (8,748,239 parameters) was worse than the baseline on PSNR and much slower.
  </p>
</CaseSection>
