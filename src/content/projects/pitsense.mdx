<CaseSection title="Objective">
  Explore tyre strategies, and search pit laps and compound sequences by total simulated race time.
  Every assumption (pace, degradation, tyre life, warm-up, fuel, pit loss) can be edited. The
  defaults are illustrative, not calibrated race predictions.
</CaseSection>
